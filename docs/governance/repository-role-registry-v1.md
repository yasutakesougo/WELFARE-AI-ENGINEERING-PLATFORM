## Export Prohibitions

Cross-repository export is prohibited for:

- credentials, tokens, secrets and authentication material;
- personal records or personally identifying data;
- child-specific observations or family history;
- customer production data;
- repository-specific identifiers represented as universal semantics without evidence;
- legally or contractually restricted content;
- unverified assumptions presented as locked knowledge.

## Local Authority Rule

WAEP is a portfolio coordination and knowledge-governance repository.

A WAEP record may say that a portable rule is `ADOPTED` or `ENFORCED` only when evidence from the target repository supports that state. WAEP does not independently grant source-code, Ready, Merge, Deploy, production, M365, SharePoint, Entra, billing or customer-delivery authority.
